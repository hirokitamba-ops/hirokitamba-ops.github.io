Overview

This document describes the installation and setup procedure for the TLIM Reproducibility Toolkit, which accompanies the TLIM Research Program. The toolkit provides the data extraction, classification, and evaluation utilities required to reproduce the empirical analyses reported in the program's preprints. Installation instructions are provided in multiple languages within the PDF.
